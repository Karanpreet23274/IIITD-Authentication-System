**Note on source quality.** Sources [8], [9] and [36] are security-conference or vendor disclosures rather than peer-reviewed papers; they are cited for the existence and nature of a publicly demonstrated attack, with the primary disclosure named and a reputable secondary report given. Vendor data sheets [41] are cited only for the cryptographic primitives a product family claims to implement, not for security assurance. Source [35] is cited only for the fact that named universities offer NFC student credentials in Apple Wallet, as stated by Apple; it is not cited as evidence of security properties.
